# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language
- Publishing others' private information (including account names, emails or credentials) without permission
- Anything that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate. To report a problem,
use GitHub's private "Report content" option or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. All
reports are handled promptly and kept confidential.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
